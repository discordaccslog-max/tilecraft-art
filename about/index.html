About Tilecraft Art

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What we make

Tilecraft Art makes original 2D pixel art packs for game developers: tilesets on a 16×16 grid, autotiling terrain, interface pieces, effects and modular characters. Every pack comes with a browser-based studio where you can look at each asset at real size, paint test maps, build characters and download the files.

Why it works this way

Choosing game art is hard when previews are polished mock-ups. So everything you see on this site is rendered from the actual files you download: the scenes on the home page, the animated previews and the sheets on each product page. What you see is what you get.

Each pack is built to be used, not just looked at. Terrain tiles join seamlessly in every combination, animations loop cleanly, and every pack ships with metadata, Tiled files and a palette so you can drop it into your project.

How the art is made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.

Because the art is drawn in code, the build script checks every pack automatically: every pixel must come from the pack's palette, every autotile must join its neighbours, every animation must loop cleanly, and every sprite must sit on its grid. Anything that fails is redrawn.
