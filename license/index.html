Who the license is for

When you buy a pack, Tilecraft Art grants you a non-exclusive license to use its assets. "You" means the buyer: either one individual, or one team or studio. Everyone on that team may use the assets for the team's projects.

What you can do

  • Use the assets in unlimited games and apps that you create, including commercial games and apps that you sell.
  • Use the assets in trailers, screenshots, store pages and other promotional material for those games and apps.
  • Modify, recolour, edit and combine the assets freely, with each other or with other art.
  • Publish your games and apps on any platform, in any number of copies.

No attribution is required. You don't need to credit Tilecraft Art anywhere, although you're welcome to.

Your finished games and apps will naturally contain the asset files. That's fine, as long as the assets are part of a playable game or app and are not offered on their own as a resource for others to use.

The license is perpetual for assets you have already downloaded. If a purchase is refunded, the license for that purchase ends, and you should stop using and delete those assets.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
