Frequently asked questions

How do license keys work?

After you pay, your order confirmation page creates your license key on the spot and shows it with a copy button. The key unlocks the pack (or packs) you bought. Keys look like TILE-, a two-letter product code, and three groups of letters and numbers.

Where do I enter my key?

On the unlock page. It's also linked from "Have a key?" at the top of every page. Once the key is accepted, your packs open in your library inside the Tilecraft Studio.

Do I need to save my key?

Yes, please copy it and keep it somewhere safe, like a password manager or a note. You'll need it to open your packs on another device or browser.

How do I download my packs?

Open a pack in the studio and go to Downloads. You can download the full pack as a .zip file, or any individual sheet at 1×, 2× or 4×.

What's in each .zip?

PNG sprite sheets and an individual PNG for every asset, each at 1×, 2× and 4× (scaled with nearest-neighbour so pixels stay sharp); a JSON file listing every asset with its position, size and animation frames and timings; for tilesets, Tiled tileset files (.tsx) with autotiling rules and finished sample maps (.tmx); the palette as .hex and .gpl files; LICENSE.txt; and README.txt with the folder layout and import notes. Each product page lists the exact contents.

Which engines and tools do the files work with?

The files use standard formats. The PNG sheets and JSON metadata can be loaded by practically any 2D engine or framework, and the .tsx and .tmx files open in Tiled and in engines that read Tiled files directly or through an importer. The README in each pack has import notes. Every engine imports differently, so please check the files with your own setup. Tilecraft Art isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any engine or tool.

How does autotiling work?

Autotiling picks the right edge or corner tile for each cell from its neighbours, so you just paint "water" or "path" and the shoreline draws itself. Terrain sets use the 47-tile "blob" layout, which covers every combination of edges and corners; fences, rivers, roads and paths use a 16-tile connection layout. The Tiled files include these rules as terrain sets, the JSON lists the neighbour mask for every tile, and the studio's map playground applies them as you paint.

Can I use the assets in commercial games?

Yes. You can use them in unlimited games and apps, including ones you sell, plus trailers, screenshots and store pages. No attribution is required. You can't resell or redistribute the assets themselves. The License page has the details.

Was the art made with AI?

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.

Can I use my packs on more than one device?

Yes. Enter the same key on each of your devices. Keys are saved in each browser, and the library has a "Sign out on this device" button when you're done on a shared computer.

What if I lose my key?

Contact us through the Contact page with your order number and the email you used at checkout, and we'll send you a replacement key.

What's the difference between Essentials and Studio Editions?

Essentials are focused packs: one tileset, kit or effect set each. Studio Editions are larger, complete systems — Ink Edition is a full matched game kit in a two-colour style, Four Seasons has six biomes with full autotiling in four seasonal palettes, and Grand Atlas adds an overworld map generator to the studio. Each product page lists the exact contents so you can compare.

Can my team use one purchase?

Yes. One purchase licenses one individual or one team or studio, and everyone on that team can use the assets in the team's projects. Please don't share your key outside your team.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of your purchase through the Contact page and include your order number — no reason needed. See the Refund Policy.
