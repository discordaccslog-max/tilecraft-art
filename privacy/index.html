Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Your license keys, saved maps, characters and settings stay in your own browser and are never sent to us or anyone else.

What stays in your browser

The site and the Tilecraft Studio use your browser's local storage, only for things you ask them to do: keeping your library unlocked on this device, remembering your saved maps, characters and generator seeds, and remembering settings such as light or dark mode and the larger-interface option. This information is stored on your device and is never transmitted. You can remove your keys with "Sign out on this device" in the library, or clear everything through your browser's site data settings.

Payments

Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider, which collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. Tilecraft Art never sees your full card details.

Order information we receive

When you buy a pack, the payment provider shares order information with us: your name, email address, the products you bought and your country. We use it only to provide support, process refunds, and meet tax and accounting obligations.

What we never do

We never sell your personal information, and we never share it for advertising.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The site's fonts are served from this site, so viewing a page doesn't contact any font service.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the EU and UK and the CCPA in California, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object to how we use it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the Contact page. We'll respond within the time the law requires.

How long we keep information

We keep order information only as long as we need it for support and refunds, and as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep transaction records. After that, we delete it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a change affects how we use order information you've already given us, we'll explain the change on this page before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us through the Contact page.
