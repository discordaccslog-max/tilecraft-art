About this pack

Grand Atlas is an overworld set at world-map scale, where each tile is a whole region: oceans with animated swell, shallows, coastlines with animated surf, plains, forests, hills, mountain ranges, deserts and snowfields, all with 47-tile autotiling. Rivers and roads connect automatically, and bridges carry roads across rivers.

Towns and landmarks are marked with small icons — villages, towns, walled cities, keeps, harbours, towers, ruins, shrines, caves, mines, lighthouses, windmills, camps and monuments — along with ships, whirlpools, waves, reefs, a compass rose and a parchment border. In the studio, the overworld generator builds new worlds from a seed, with sliders for land, mountains, forests, rivers and towns, and exports them as PNG images and Tiled maps.

Why it's a Studio Edition

Grand Atlas is the only pack with world-map scale terrain: 9 autotiling systems (shallows, land, desert, snowfield, hills, forest, mountains, river, road) and 386 assets designed for overworld maps.

It also adds a tool to the studio: a seeded overworld generator that builds new worlds on demand with controls for land, mountains, forests, rivers and towns, and exports them as PNG images and Tiled maps. Same seed, same world — every time.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
