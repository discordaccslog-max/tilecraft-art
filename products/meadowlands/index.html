About this pack

Meadowlands is an outdoor tileset for farms, fields and forest edges. Dirt, path and water each come as a complete 47-tile autotiling set, so every shoreline, corner and narrow channel has its own tile. Water is animated, with moving ripples and foam along the bank, and the terrain sets sit on transparent surroundings so you can layer them over any ground.

Around them: eight grass ground tiles, swaying tall grass and reeds, six flower patches, rocks and a boulder, four bushes, five trees including a pine and a blossom tree, a stump and a log, a 16-piece connecting fence with gates, wooden bridge pieces, lily pads, stepping stones and two fluttering butterflies.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
