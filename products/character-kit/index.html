About this pack

The Modular Character Kit is a layered character system. Pick a body (slim or sturdy) in one of six skin tones, add one of ten outfits in three colourways, one of eight hairstyles in six colours, and accessories such as hats, glasses, a scarf or a backpack. Every layer lines up frame for frame.

Each layer has a four-frame walk and a two-frame idle in four directions (down, left, right, up) on 16×32 frames. The studio's character builder lets you combine layers and export a finished sprite sheet, and the zip includes a ready-to-use sheet for every single layer.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
