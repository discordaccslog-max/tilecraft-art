About this pack

Effects & Particles is a set of animated sprite sheets for game feel: sparkles in four colours, dust puffs, smoke puffs and a looping smoke plume, magic swirls, item pickup flashes, water and goo splashes, impact flashes and level-up bursts.

Looping effects loop without a jump, and one-shot effects fade to almost nothing on their last frame so they can be replayed back to back. There are also particle sprites — dots in three sizes and four colours, streaks, glints, rings, a snowflake, a tumbling leaf, an ember and a bubble — for your engine's particle system.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
