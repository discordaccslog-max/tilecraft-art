About this pack

Ink Edition is a complete, standalone game kit drawn in a strict two-colour style: paper and ink. Every part was designed together — outdoor terrain with autotiling paths and animated water, an autotiling dungeon with props, a town with modular houses, 22 items, a full interface set and effects.

It includes its own layered character system with bodies in three tones, outfits, hair and accessories, all with four-direction walk and idle animations, and it works in the studio's character builder. Because every pixel is one of two colours, you can recolour the whole kit by swapping those two colours — the studio has a live recolour control.

Why it's a Studio Edition

Ink Edition is a complete, matched kit rather than a single tileset: 655 assets covering terrain, a dungeon, a town, 22 items, interface pieces, effects and a full layered character system.

Everything shares one two-colour style, so pieces from different parts of the kit always sit together, and the whole kit recolours by swapping two colours — live in the studio.

For comparison, each Essentials pack covers one area: Meadowlands has 208 outdoor assets, Stonehollow 182 dungeon assets.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
