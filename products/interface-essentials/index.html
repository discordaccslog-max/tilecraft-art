About this pack

Interface Essentials gives your game a full set of interface pieces in three matching themes — Dusk (indigo with brass trim), Vellum (parchment and ink) and Moss (green stone with copper). Panels, windows, frames, dialogue boxes, slots, bars, tabs and buttons are built as nine-slice pieces, and the JSON lists the slice margins for each one, so they stretch cleanly to any size.

Buttons come in normal, hover, pressed and disabled states; inventory slots in normal, hover, selected and disabled. There are health, energy and experience bar fills (smooth and segmented), toggles, checkboxes, radio buttons, slider and scrollbar parts, speech tails, a continue arrow, 12 cursors with hotspots and 19 icons.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
