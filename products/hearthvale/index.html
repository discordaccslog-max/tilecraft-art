About this pack

Hearthvale is a town tileset with modular buildings. Walls come in plaster with timber framing, cut stone and wooden planks; roofs in clay tiles, slate and thatch. Each is a set of left, middle and right pieces, so houses can be any width. Doors, windows (including lit ones for evening scenes) and chimneys are separate overlays you place on top.

Around the houses: cobblestone paths and plaza slabs with full 47-tile autotiling, three market stalls, a well, an animated lamppost, a notice board and signposts with blank boards, a hay cart, crates, barrels, sacks, hay bales, benches, planters, trees, shrubs and a connecting picket fence.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
