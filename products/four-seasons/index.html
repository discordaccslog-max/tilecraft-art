About this pack

Four Seasons is a terrain studio: meadow, forest, desert, snowfield, swamp and coast, each with ground tiles, a land autotiling set (dirt, underbrush, mesa cliffs, tundra grass, mud or dune grass) and an animated water autotiling set, all using the full 47-tile layout.

Everything comes in spring, summer, autumn and winter palettes drawn from one shared master palette, so trees turn gold in autumn and snow settles in winter while every tile still joins its neighbours. Each biome also has decor and animated foliage, and every biome comes with a finished sample map in each season.

Why it's a Studio Edition

Meadowlands has one biome, 4 autotile sets and one palette. Four Seasons has six biomes, 12 full 47-tile autotile sets and four seasonal palettes — 635 assets drawn once and delivered as 2,540 asset files.

Every season comes with its own Tiled tilesets and a finished sample map for every biome, and the studio switches seasons live in the preview and the map playground.

How these assets are made

The art in these packs was created with AI assistance and drawn programmatically in code, pixel by pixel. No AI image generators were used, and no one else's artwork was used or traced.

Some game stores and platforms ask developers to disclose AI-assisted content in their games. If you publish a game that uses these assets, follow the rules of the store or platform you publish on.
